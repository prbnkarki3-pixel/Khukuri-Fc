Our Story

From Nepalese roots to Aotearoa.

Khukuri FC was formed by the Nepalese community in Auckland and has grown from football participation into a wider community platform. The khukuri symbol connects heritage, courage, honour and identity.

Today the club connects senior football, Masters, youth development, tournaments, cultural events and community initiatives.

“It takes a village to raise a champion.”

Culture + Community

Football connects cultures.

A Khukuri matchday is not only about ninety minutes. It is family, food, language, music, volunteering, youth, celebration and the shared experience of being Nepali in Aotearoa.
